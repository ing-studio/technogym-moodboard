Gym design, a mood board method

A mood is built from the whole building

A mood board is often a set of beautiful rooms. We build ours the other way round: from what was proposed, through everything it has to answer, down to the plan of every floor.

We start with the proposal.

What is proposed

A styling made to present the equipment

Technogym proposes a generic styling. Its purpose is to show the equipment line at its best, so each image is a single ideal room, seen at eye level, in a different setting.

What it offers

A calm, warm direction: sand-coloured machines, natural timber, soft daylight.

It shows rooms. A gym is a building, so we need to look further.

What we need to look at

Read it in layers

A render shows one room at eye level. Every picture holds four layers, and each one is a separate decision.

  1. 1Shell: ceiling, walls and floor
  2. 2Light: daylight and artificial light
  3. 3Equipment: the Technogym line
  4. 4Objects: storage, rugs, art and planting

Only layer 3 comes from Technogym. Everything else is designed for this building.

Layers explain a room. To see how the rooms work together, we look from above.

What needs to be assessed

Four points to assess before we build the mood

From above, three more aspects appear: zones, route and depth. Read together with the layers, they raise four points. None of them rules the styling out.

  1. 1DaylightLight, depth
    Every render relies on large windows and a view. Deep and lower-ground floors have neither, so the mood cannot depend on daylight.
  2. 2DensityEquipment, zones
    The renders show a few machines in open space. The plan places rows of stations, so the calm has to survive a full floor.
  3. 3ContinuityRoute
    Each render is a separate room in a different place. Members move through one sequence, so the mood has to hold from the entrance to the last studio.
  4. 4CoverageZones, route
    Only training is shown. Arrival, changing rooms and circulation are on the plan and still need a styling.

The mood we suggest answers all four together: every layer, every zone, every floor.

That is why the plan comes before the mood.
